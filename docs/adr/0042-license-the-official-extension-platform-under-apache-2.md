# License the official extension platform under Apache-2.0

Status: accepted

The T3 Extension Host, installer, official catalog metadata, Jordan Extension Stack preset, and Jordan-authored Extension Bundles will be distributed under Apache-2.0. This keeps the reusable platform permissive while providing an explicit patent grant. Every third-party catalog entry must expose its Extension Distribution License before installation and prove that the catalog is permitted to redistribute its source and artifacts. Third-party extensions may use a different source-available license, but the official catalog will refuse missing, ambiguous, or distribution-incompatible licensing.

## Considered Options

- MIT for all official work: simple and permissive, but lacks Apache-2.0's explicit patent grant.
- Require Apache-2.0 for every third-party extension: maximizes uniformity, but unnecessarily excludes otherwise distributable extensions.
- Use Apache-2.0 for the official platform and disclose compatible third-party licenses: preserves broad reuse, patent clarity, and honest adopter choice.
